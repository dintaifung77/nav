The Three Keys (Mark 1:35)

T

TIME

  • Even 5 minutes is better than none
  • Consistency is key
  • Morning, lunch, after class, before bed?
P

PLACE

  • Find where you're not distracted
  • Bedroom, cafe, or at school
  • Jesus chose "a solitary place"
P

PLAN

  • Choose your Bible passage
  • Meditate, memorize, or research
  • Journal your reflections

The ABCs of Bible Reading

A

ASK Questions

  • Who is speaking? To whom? Why?
  • What's the main point?
  • What can I apply today?
  • What is God saying to me?
B

BEST Verse

  • Find one key verse
  • Read it again or use in prayer
  • Listen to the Holy Spirit
  • Think about it throughout the day
C

COMMIT

  • Choose one area to commit to God
  • Work, test, meeting, or attitude
  • "Lord, I trust You with this"
